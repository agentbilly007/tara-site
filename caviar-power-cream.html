What's inside

Key ingredients

  • 150-hour fermented caviar oil. Fermentation concentrates the caviar's antioxidants.
  • Omega-3 and omega-6 fatty acids. From the caviar complex; they help skin look smoother and more refined.
  • Hawaiian deep sea water. Mineral-dense water for lasting hydration.
  • Diamond powder. Finely milled for a soft, luminous finish.

Paraben-free. No artificial fragrance or added pigment.

How to use

Step by step

  1. Use as the final step of your routine, morning or night.
  2. Warm a small amount between your fingertips.
  3. Press gently over the face and neck, avoiding the eye area.

Questions

Is it too heavy for daytime?

It's rich, so a small amount goes a long way. Many people use it at night and the Algae Marine Lotion in the morning.

What does the diamond powder do?

It's finely milled and gives the skin a soft, luminous finish.
