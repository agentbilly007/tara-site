What's inside

Key ingredients

  • Icelandic glacial water. The base of the formula.
  • Undaria Pinnatifida sea algae, hydrolyzed fucoidan and Bacillus ferment. Marine ingredients for deep hydration.
  • Niacinamide. Helps even the look of skin tone and texture.
  • Squalane and trehalose. Hold moisture in place and soften the look of fine lines.

Paraben-free. Cruelty-free. No artificial fragrance or added pigment. Suitable for sensitive skin.

How to use

Step by step

  1. After toner, dispense a small amount.
  2. Press evenly over the face and neck.
  3. Follow with cream.
  4. Use morning and night.

Questions

What is an emulsion?

An emulsion is the lotion step in a Korean routine: lighter than a cream, it sits between toner and cream and seals in the layers underneath.

Is it enough on its own?

For oily or combination skin, many people use it as their only moisturizer. Dry skin can follow with the Caviar Power Cream.
