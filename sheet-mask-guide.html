How to use a Korean sheet mask for dry skin

Sheet masks are one of the easiest steps in a Korean routine. A thin sheet holds a concentrated essence against your skin for about twenty minutes, so the moisture has time to sink in instead of evaporating. Here's how to get the most out of one.

When to use it in your routine

Use a sheet mask after cleansing and toner, and before lotion and cream. If you use a serum or ampoule, you can apply it before the mask or skip it on mask nights. The mask essence does the same job.

Step by step

  1. Cleanse and tone. Start with clean skin so the essence isn't sitting on top of makeup or sunscreen.
  2. Unfold and place. Line up the eye holes first, then smooth the sheet outward and press gently around the nose and chin to remove air bubbles.
  3. Wait about 20 minutes. Don't leave it on until it dries out completely.
  4. Remove and pat. Peel the sheet off and pat the leftover essence into your face and neck. Don't rinse.
  5. Seal it in. Follow with lotion and cream while skin is still damp.

How often?

Two or three times a week works well for most people. Many people like a mask the night before an event, or after a long flight when skin feels tight.

What to look for in a mask for dry skin

Look for hydrating ingredients: hyaluronic acid, algae and seaweed extracts, glycerin and ceramides. Fragrance-free formulas are a good choice if your skin is easily irritated.
