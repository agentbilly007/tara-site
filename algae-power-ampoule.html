What's inside

Key ingredients

  • Marine fermented nano water. The base: fermented marine water that absorbs quickly.
  • Four seaweed extracts. Hizikia Fusiforme, Ecklonia Cava, Laminaria Japonica and Undaria Pinnatifida, rich in antioxidants.
  • Yeast ferment and niacinamide. Help skin look brighter and more even.
  • Ceramide NP, squalane and shea butter. Add moisture and leave skin feeling soft and smooth.
  • Sodium hyaluronate. Holds hydration so skin looks plumper and less tired.

Paraben-free. Cruelty-free. No artificial fragrance or added pigment.

How to use

Step by step

  1. After toner, apply two or three drops.
  2. Press gently into the face and neck.
  3. Follow with lotion or cream.
  4. Use morning and night.

Questions

What's the difference between an ampoule and a serum?

In Korean skincare an ampoule is a more concentrated serum. You only need two or three drops.

Can I use it on mask nights?

Yes, apply it before the sheet mask, or skip it that night since the mask essence does a similar job.
