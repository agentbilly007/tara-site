The routine

The TARA Korean skincare routine, step by step

A Korean routine layers products from thinnest to thickest so each one can sink in before the next. Here is the order for the TARA collection, morning and night. You don't need every step every day: toner, lotion and cream are the core, and the rest are add-ons.

The order

  1. Algae Marine Toner: Cleanse, then press in the toner. Morning and night. Use a cotton pad or your palms.
  2. Algae Power Ampoule: Two or three drops of ampoule. Press into the face and neck.
  3. Algae Ampoule Sea Therapy Mask: Sheet mask, two or three times a week. 20 minutes, then pat in the leftover essence.
  4. Algae Marine Lotion: Lotion while skin is still damp. Seals in the layers underneath without weight.
  5. Black Caviar Power Eyecream: Eye cream around the orbital bone. Pat with your ring finger, sweep the rest down the neck.
  6. Caviar Power Cream: Finish with cream. A small amount, warmed between your fingertips.
  7. BHA Overnight Velvet Peel: At night, two or three times a week: BHA peel pad. Right after cleansing on peel nights, then moisturize. Sunscreen the next morning.

Morning vs. night

Morning: toner, ampoule, lotion, eye cream, then sunscreen. Keep it light.

Night: toner, ampoule, lotion, eye cream and cream. On mask nights, add the sheet mask after the ampoule. On peel nights, use the BHA pad right after cleansing and skip the ampoule and mask that night.

Starting out?

If you're new to Korean skincare, start with three steps: toner, lotion and the sheet mask twice a week. Add the rest once you know how your skin responds.
