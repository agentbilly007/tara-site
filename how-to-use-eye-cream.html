How to use eye cream the right way (eyes and neck)

To use eye cream the right way, take a small amount on your ring finger and pat it gently around the orbital bone, the ring of bone around your eye. Do it morning and night, before your face cream, and take what's left down your neck. Here's each part in a little more detail.

How much eye cream to use

Start small. A little goes a long way on this delicate skin, and a thick layer only takes longer to sink in. Begin with a small dab for both eyes. If the skin still feels dry once it has absorbed, add a touch more.

How to apply it, step by step

  1. Start with clean skin. Cleanse, then apply your lighter layers such as toner, ampoule or serum, and lotion.
  2. Use your ring finger. It makes it easier to keep a light touch.
  3. Dot along the orbital bone. Place a few small dots under the eye and toward the outer corner.
  4. Pat, don't rub. Tap gently until it spreads into a thin layer. Avoid pulling or dragging the skin.
  5. Keep it out of your eyes. Stay on the bone and leave space along the lash line. If any gets in your eyes, rinse with water.
  6. Finish on your neck. Sweep what's left on your fingers down the neck.

Should you put eye cream on your neck?

It depends on the product, so check the directions. Some eye creams are made for the eye area only. Ours is made for both the eyes and the neck, and the neck is a spot that's easy to skip. Use light strokes there. If the leftover doesn't cover your neck comfortably, take a little more.

When does eye cream go in your routine?

In the TARA routine, eye cream comes after lotion and before face cream. In the morning, follow your routine with sunscreen. At night, finish with cream. If you use the Caviar Power Cream as your last step, keep it off the eye area and let your eye cream cover that spot.

On nights when you use an exfoliating acid pad, keep the pad away from your eyes.

Using eye cream under makeup

In the morning, use a thin layer and give it a minute to absorb before concealer. Then pat your concealer on with the same light touch.

Common mistakes

  • Using too much at once.
  • Rubbing or tugging instead of patting.
  • Applying it right up to the lash line.
  • Putting a heavier face cream on first.
