What's inside

Key ingredients

  • BHA (5,000 ppm) and AHA. Work overnight to lift away dulling buildup so skin and pores look smoother and more refined.
  • Marine collagen and hyaluronic acid. Keep skin looking replenished rather than tight or stripped.
  • Seawater. Part of the buffering base.

Paraben-free. No artificial fragrance or added pigment. For evening use.

How to use

Step by step

  1. At night, after cleansing, wipe one pad evenly across dry skin, avoiding the eye area.
  2. Leave on. Don't rinse.
  3. Follow with your usual moisturizer.
  4. Use two or three nights a week.
  5. Wear sunscreen the next morning.

Questions

Can I use it every night?

Start two or three nights a week and see how your skin feels.

Do I need sunscreen after?

Yes. Acids can make skin more sensitive to the sun, so wear sunscreen the following morning.
