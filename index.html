The routine

A simple Korean routine

  1. Cleanse, then press in the Algae Marine Toner.
  2. Two or three drops of the Algae Power Ampoule.
  3. Sheet mask for 20 minutes, two or three times a week.
  4. Lotion while skin is still damp.
  5. Seal with cream, and tap eye cream around the orbital bone.
