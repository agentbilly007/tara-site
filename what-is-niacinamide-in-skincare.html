Guide

What is niacinamide in skincare?

Niacinamide is a form of vitamin B3. It dissolves in water, so you'll find it in toners, serums, ampoules, lotions and creams. It's a common ingredient in Korean skincare because it fits easily into a daily routine.

What does niacinamide do for the look of skin?

In cosmetics, niacinamide is mostly chosen for how skin looks. It's often used to help:

  • skin tone look more even
  • dull skin look brighter
  • skin texture look smoother

Think of it as a steady, everyday ingredient. Changes in how skin looks tend to be gradual, so give a product regular use before you judge it. Results vary from person to person.

How do you find it on a label?

Look for the word "niacinamide" in the ingredient list. It's related to niacin, but they're different ingredients with different names, so check the spelling. A formula is more than one ingredient, so also look at what comes with it, such as ingredients that add moisture if your skin feels dry.

Where does it go in your routine?

That depends on the product, not the ingredient. Apply your products from thinnest to thickest: toner, then serum or ampoule, then lotion and cream. You can use a niacinamide product morning or night, following the directions on its package. In the morning, finish with sunscreen.

Can you use it with other ingredients?

Niacinamide is usually easy to pair with hydrating ingredients such as hyaluronate, ceramides and squalane. Many people also use it in the same routine as vitamin C. If you use exfoliating acids like AHA or BHA, add one new product at a time so you can tell how your skin responds to each.

Do you need more than one niacinamide product?

No. One product you enjoy using regularly is a fine place to start. More layers aren't necessarily better, and a short routine is easier to keep up. If you do use several, watch how your skin feels and cut back if it seems uncomfortable.

How to try it for the first time

  1. Patch test. Try a little on a small area first and wait a day.
  2. Start simply. Add only this one new product to your routine.
  3. Pay attention. If you notice stinging, itching or redness that bothers you, stop using it.

If you have a concern about your skin, ask a doctor.
