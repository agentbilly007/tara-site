What's inside

Key ingredients

  • Icelandic glacial water. The base of the formula, carrying everything else into the skin.
  • Undaria Pinnatifida sea algae and hydrolyzed fucoidan. Marine ingredients for hydration that leaves skin looking fresh.
  • Niacinamide. Helps even the look of skin tone.
  • Centella Asiatica and madecassoside. Help reduce the look of redness.
  • Papaya, plum, apple and grape extracts. An antioxidant fruit blend for a fresher, more luminous look.

Paraben-free. Cruelty-free. No artificial fragrance or added pigment. Suitable for sensitive skin. KFDA registered.

How to use

Step by step

  1. After cleansing, dispense onto a cotton pad or into your palms.
  2. Press evenly over the face and neck.
  3. Follow with the ampoule, lotion and cream.
  4. Use morning and night.

Questions

Is this a toner or an essence?

It's a concentrated ampoule toner: it does the job of a toner as the first step after cleansing, with a richer, more concentrated formula than a basic toner.

Can I use it with a cotton pad?

Yes. You can sweep it on with a cotton pad or press it in with your palms.
