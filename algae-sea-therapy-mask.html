Algae Ampoule Sea Therapy Mask

The easiest place to start. A five-sheet set soaked in a green spirulina ampoule from marine algae. Twenty minutes and skin looks hydrated, plump and brighter.

What's inside

Key ingredients

  • Spirulina ampoule. A 100% natural spirulina ampoule drawn from marine algae, the green essence each sheet is saturated with.
  • Deep sea water. Mineral-rich water for a concentrated dose of moisture.
  • Pearl extract. For a brighter, more even-looking complexion.
  • Centella Asiatica. A Korean skincare classic, gentle on sensitive skin.

Paraben-free. No artificial fragrance or added pigment. Suitable for sensitive skin.

How to use

Step by step

  1. Cleanse and tone.
  2. Unfold the sheet and smooth it over your face, pressing gently around the nose and chin.
  3. Leave on for 20 minutes.
  4. Remove and pat the remaining essence into your skin. No need to rinse.
  5. Use two or three times a week.

Questions

How long do I leave the sheet mask on?

About 20 minutes. Don't leave it on until it dries out.

Do I rinse after?

No. Pat the leftover essence into your skin, then follow with lotion and cream.
