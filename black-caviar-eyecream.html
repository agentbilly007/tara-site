How to use

Step by step

  1. Morning and night, take a small amount on your ring finger.
  2. Pat gently around the orbital bone.
  3. Sweep what's left down the neck.
  4. Apply before your face cream.

Questions

Why use it on the neck too?

The eye area and the neck are the two places that tend to show age first, and the neck gets skipped most often.

Will it crease under concealer?

It's made to be lightweight so it doesn't sit heavy or crease. Give it a minute to absorb before makeup.
