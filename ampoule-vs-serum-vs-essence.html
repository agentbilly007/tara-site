Guide

Ampoule vs. serum vs. essence: what's the difference?

Korean skincare has three words that confuse almost everyone at first: essence, serum and ampoule. They're all light, watery-to-silky liquids that go on after toner and before moisturizer. The difference is mostly about how concentrated they are and how much you use.

Essence

The lightest of the three. An essence is thin and watery, made to add a first layer of hydration. Many Korean toners now work like essences, so you often don't need both. Our Algae Marine Toner is a concentrated "ampoule toner" that fills both roles.

Serum

A step more concentrated. Serums usually focus on one or two goals, like hydration or a more even-looking tone, and you use a few drops.

Ampoule

The most concentrated. In Korean skincare an ampoule is a highly concentrated serum, named after the small sealed vials these formulas were once sold in. Because it's concentrated, two or three drops are enough. Our Algae Power Ampoule is built on marine fermented water with four seaweed extracts, niacinamide, ceramide NP and sodium hyaluronate.

Do you need all three?

No. Pick one or two. A simple routine is toner, then a serum or ampoule, then lotion or cream. If your skin looks dull or feels tight, an ampoule is a good one to start with.

What order do they go in?

Thinnest to thickest: toner or essence, then serum, then ampoule, then lotion and cream. If you use a sheet mask, it goes after the ampoule. See the full TARA routine.

How to apply an ampoule

  1. After toner, put two or three drops in your palm.
  2. Press, don't rub. Press it gently into the face and neck.
  3. Wait a moment, then follow with lotion or cream.
